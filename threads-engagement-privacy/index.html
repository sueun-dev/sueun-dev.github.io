Thread Be Famous Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Thread Be Famous is a tool operated by Sueun Cho that helps the owner of a connected Threads account find public Threads posts on topics they follow, prepare reply drafts, and publish a reply only after the owner reviews and approves it. It is not offered as a public service, and it does not ask other people to sign in.

Information we use

  • Connected account: the Threads user ID, username, and an OAuth access token for the account that connects to the app.
  • Public posts: text, post ID, permalink, timestamp, media type, and author username of public Threads posts returned by keyword search or by public profile lookup.
  • Replies: the text and IDs of replies drafted and published by the connected account, and public replies on posts the connected account owns.

How we use it

Information is used only to show candidate posts to the account owner, draft a reply, and publish a reply that the owner expressly approves. We do not use the information for advertising, profiling, or building audiences, and we do not sell, rent, or share it with data brokers or advertising networks.

Storage, retention, and security

Data is stored only on the account owner's own device, in local configuration and queue files. Access tokens are never published, logged in reports, or sent to third parties. Candidate posts and drafts are kept only as long as the owner needs them to review and track replies, and the owner can delete them at any time.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time in Threads under Settings > Account > Website permissions. To ask us to delete any data related to you, follow the data deletion instructions or email us.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email sueun.dev@gmail.com.
