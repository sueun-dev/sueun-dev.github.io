sueun-api TikTok Uploader

sueun-api is an owner-operated desktop tool that uploads short vertical videos I create to my own TikTok account, either as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as direct posts.

  • Sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) to connect an account.
  • Before every direct post, the tool shows the account's nickname, the allowed privacy levels, and comment, duet and stitch options, and you choose them yourself.
  • Videos are uploaded only from files on your own computer, only when you run the tool.
