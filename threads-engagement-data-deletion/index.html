Thread Be Famous Data Deletion Instructions

Remove the app's access

  1. Open Threads and go to Settings > Account > Website permissions.
  2. Select Thread Be Famous and choose Remove.

Ask us to delete your data

Email sueun.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include your Threads username. Within 30 days we will delete the stored access token, account name, candidate posts, reply drafts, and related records, and we will confirm by email.
