sueun-api Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

sueun-api is an owner-operated desktop tool that uploads videos to TikTok. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What we access

  • Basic profile information from TikTok (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Creator information needed to post (nickname, allowed privacy levels, comment/duet/stitch settings, maximum video length).
  • The video files and captions you choose to upload.

How it is stored

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on your own computer. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's API.
  • We do not run a server, and we do not collect, sell, or share your data with third parties.
  • Videos are sent directly from your computer to TikTok.

Deleting your data

Delete the local token file, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions. To ask about your data, email sueun.dev@gmail.com.

See also the Terms of Service.
